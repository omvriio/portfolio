## 🔄 **How the Circular Rotation Works:**

### **1. The Math Behind It** (Lines 23-29)
```jsx
const handleSegmentClick = (segment) => {
  // Calculate rotation needed to center segment at 0° (right horizontal)
  const targetRotation = -segment.angle
  setExpandedSegment(segment, targetRotation)
}
```

**Example:**
- **About segment** is at `45°` → Rotate wheel by `-45°` to bring it to `0°` (horizontal right)
- **Work segment** is at `135°` → Rotate wheel by `-135°` to bring it to `0°`
- **Skills segment** is at `225°` → Rotate wheel by `-225°` to bring it to `0°`
- **Contact segment** is at `315°` → Rotate wheel by `-315°` to bring it to `0°`

### **2. The Animation** (Lines 64-68)
```jsx
<motion.svg 
  animate={{
    rotate: wheelRotation  // This value comes from the store
  }}
  transition={{ duration: 0.8, type: 'spring', stiffness: 80 }}
>
```

The `motion.svg` from Framer Motion:
- Takes the `wheelRotation` value (e.g., `-45`, `-135`, etc.)
- **Smoothly rotates** the entire SVG element around its center
- Uses a **spring animation** (bouncy, natural feel) with stiffness 80
- Duration of 0.8 seconds for the rotation

### **3. How SVG Rotation Works**

The SVG has a center point at `(0, 0)` because of this viewBox:
```jsx
viewBox={`${-svgSize/2} ${-svgSize/2} ${svgSize} ${svgSize}`}
```

When you rotate the SVG:
- All segments rotate **together** as one unit
- They stay in their relative positions
- The clicked segment ends up horizontal (at 0°/360°)

### **5. The Complete Flow**

1. **User clicks** "Skills" segment
2. `handleSegmentClick` calculates: `targetRotation = -225°`
3. Store updates: `wheelRotation` becomes `-225`
4. `motion.svg` detects the change
5. SVG **smoothly animates** from current rotation to `-225°`
6. Skills segment now sits horizontally on the right
7. Details panel appears next to it

### **Key Points:**
- ✅ **Negative rotation** brings segments clockwise to 0°
- ✅ **Spring animation** gives it a natural, bouncy feel
- ✅ **All segments rotate together** because they're inside the same SVG
- ✅ **No complex calculations** - just simple angle negation!

It's elegant because it leverages SVG's built-in rotation around a center point, combined with Framer Motion's smooth animation capabilities!
